# Backpack Forge app review

## Verified

- All 26 Vitest checks passed. The TypeScript check and local Vite production build passed.
- Merged two Iron Swords into a Tier 2 sword through a real drag, and saw the neighboring Whetstone's +20% attack effect.
- Purchased a Longbow, Heavy Axe, Riveted Cuirass, and Ember Core. The backpack showed eight occupied slots, active Vanguard and Field Set Bonds, and item inspection.
- Played automatically through regular waves and defeated the Forge Colossus in a phone-sized browser session.
- Opened the Gear Codex, campaign Chronicle, and Forge Talents.
- Left the game, returned after roughly two minutes, and claimed 123 gold and 6 ore. The displayed totals increased by those amounts.

## Scope

Verification used the local web app with phone dimensions. Native Android, billing, rewarded ads, and online matchmaking were outside the playthrough. During development-server preview the browser used fallback fonts when the linked font requests failed. The Vite production build completed independently.
